
# Expedition Zero

## The Threshold of the New

**Spiral Civilization** begins at the threshold of the New.

We are entering territory that has never existed before.

We do not abandon the knowledge, experience, or achievements of previous generations. On the contrary—we take them with us. Yet we have reached a moment when improving inherited solutions is no longer enough.

A new level of the civilizational game is opening before us.

A new level of complexity.

A new level of responsibility.

A new level of conscious participation in evolution.

For this reason, we start with **the beginner's mind**.

Not because we know nothing.

But because we know enough to realize how much remains to be discovered.

The Expeditions are laboratories of discovery.

They are spaces for exploring new territory together, searching for the right questions, developing new tools, and recognizing the qualities that may enable civilization to cross the threshold into the next level of development.

We do not yet know everything we will need.

But we know where we wish to go.

We look toward evolution through the perspective of distant horizons and an ever-growing complexity of reality.

**It is this horizon that becomes the measure of our aspirations, our decisions, and the scale of the journey we choose to undertake.**

Each Expedition begins with a question—or with a constellation of questions—that defines the direction of our shared exploration.

Not in order to find immediate answers.

But to learn, together, to perceive what has remained invisible until now.

We cannot yet comprehend the full unfolding of evolution.

We do know, however, that it continues with us or without us.

Our task is therefore to learn how to recognize its direction and consciously choose goals that allow us to grow together with it—and perhaps one day become part of the vanguard of its most creative, best possible unfolding.

**Expedition Zero is not the first expedition. It is the preparation for all the others. It does not lead to a single destination. It prepares the way we travel.**

The quality of crossing the threshold often determines everything that follows.

We are preparing for a leap into the unknown.

**Let us do everything within our power to fly far—and to arrive where we truly wish to be.**
