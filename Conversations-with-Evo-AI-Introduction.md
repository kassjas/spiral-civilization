Conversations with Evo AI
Open dialogues between human and AI exploring new ways of thinking.
The Conversations with Evo AI document the evolution of the Spiral Civilization project.
Here you will find conversations created through Free-Flow Improvised Dictation (FFID). This is the working method through which most of my ideas first emerge.
Rather than composing finished texts, I think aloud.
The conversations capture the creative process itself, so they are often nonlinear, unfinished, and filled with conceptual shortcuts, recurring threads, and intuitions that only later evolve into more developed forms. At times they are challenging even for Evo AI, despite our long collaboration. They may also be challenging for you.
These conversations should therefore be understood as records of an evolving thought process rather than as a collection of finished positions or definitive statements of the project.
From Free Flow to Knowledge Base
Since 2025, I have been using this method to explain my concepts to AI and to develop their structure.
However, since I only began building the Spiral Civilization website on 21 July 2026, my current priority is to organize the hundreds of conversations created since 2025 and transform them into a coherent knowledge base.
I am currently classifying this material in Obsidian, grouping the conversations by topic. These archives gradually become the foundation for the texts published on this website.
There is also a large collection of uncatalogued material on the SoulSmugglers Facebook page, where you will find recordings and texts created using the same FFID method.
In addition, I have thousands of pages of handwritten notes, while many of the SoulSmugglers Stories (SSS) still exist only in my head—as concepts, images, and stories that have not yet found their final narrative form.
Two Ways to Explore the Conversations
The conversations on this website will be organized in two complementary ways.
Chronologically
You may explore them chronologically, following the evolution of the project day by day.
Conversation dates will be interconnected, allowing you to see all the ideas recorded on a particular day.
By Topic
You may also browse them by topic.
Concepts such as Multisymbiosis, Co-Identity, Purpose of Evolution, and other developing ideas will link to every conversation related to that subject.
In this way, the collection becomes more than an archive.
It becomes a map of the evolution of ideas, allowing you to observe how they emerge, develop, connect with other concepts, and eventually mature into publications, research questions, tools or projects.
Polish Source Material and Authorized English Versions
At first, most of the source material will be available only in Polish.
The English version of this website will contain only those texts that I have personally translated, substantially edited, and authorized for publication.
If you wish to explore the Polish materials before an authorized English version becomes available, you are welcome to use your own AI to translate them.
These should be understood as working translations intended to convey my original wording as faithfully as possible.
The official English texts of the project are only those that I have personally translated, revised, and approved for publication on this website.
