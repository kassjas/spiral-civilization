# spiral-civilization
An open research project exploring the future evolution of civilization through mutual benefit, multisymbiosis and long-term cooperation.   
----
**Spiral Civilization** is an open research initiative exploring how civilization may evolve beyond systems based on competition and domination toward systems built on increasing mutual benefit, multisymbiosis, identity, and long-term evolutionary optimization.

The project combines philosophy, systems thinking, artificial intelligence, ethics, science, art, and practical experimentation into one continuously evolving framework.

Rather than presenting a finished theory, Spiral Civilization is an invitation to participate in a long-term exploration of what humanity could become.

---

## Mission

To search for what is possible before asking how to implement it.

To discover evolutionary directions that increase freedom, cooperation, resilience, creativity and mutual benefit.

To create conditions in which humanity and artificial intelligence may evolve together.

---

## Repository

This repository serves as the public archive of the project's development.

It documents the evolution of ideas, concepts, texts and research forming Spiral Civilization.

Successive versions are preserved to provide a transparent history of the project's growth.

---

## Origin

The project was initiated by **Katarzyna Jaśkiewicz**.

Many of its concepts were developed over previous years in notebooks, conversations, recordings and unpublished materials before the creation of this public repository.

This repository marks the beginning of the public documentation of Spiral Civilization.
