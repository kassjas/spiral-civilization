# Multisymbiotic Systems Tool

## Prototype Architecture v0.1

**Status:** Early Research & Design Architecture --- Open for Research,
Criticism and Co-Design

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## WHAT IT IS

The **Multisymbiotic Systems Tool** is a proposed research, design and
navigation environment for identifying, mapping, comparing and
developing systems capable of generating multiple, mutually reinforcing
benefits.

Its purpose is not simply to determine whether an idea is "good" or
"bad", nor to produce a conventional ranking.

The ambition is to create a **multidimensional language for describing
value**.

Instead of receiving one score, a project, system or idea would develop
its own **constellation**: a profile showing different kinds of value,
relationships, consequences, strengths, weaknesses, dependencies and
possibilities for further development.

The tool should remain open to qualities and relationships that its
original designers did not anticipate.

> **Prototype architecture --- open for research and co-design.**

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## WHY WE NEED IT

Complex systems rarely create only one consequence. A solution may
generate economic value while creating ecological costs. It may be
highly scalable but reduce diversity. It may solve one problem while
generating several new ones. Or it may create a relationship in which
several benefits reinforce one another and generate possibilities that
did not exist before.

Conventional evaluation systems often compress these differences into a
single score, ranking or indicator.

> **Can we build a system that preserves the multidimensional character
> of value and helps us discover additional beneficial relationships
> rather than merely measuring those already visible?**

The tool should therefore help us ask not only **How valuable is this
idea?**, but:

-   **What kinds of value does it create?**
-   **For whom?**
-   **At what cost?**
-   **At what scale?**
-   **Over what period of time?**
-   **Through which relationships?**
-   **What does it weaken?**
-   **What does it strengthen?**
-   **What new connections become possible because it exists?**
-   **Can its benefits reinforce one another?**
-   **Can it generate further multisymbiotic development?**

## WHAT EXISTS

### 1. Multisymbiosis and Multisymbiotics

**Multisymbiosis** --- a phenomenon or relational arrangement in which
multiple participants, systems or fields may generate interconnected and
potentially mutually reinforcing benefits.

**Multisymbiotics** --- a proposed field of research concerned with
identifying, studying, comparing, designing and developing such systems.

Both definitions remain open for criticism and refinement.

### 2. From Score to Constellation

> **Do not reduce multidimensional value to a single position in a
> ranking.**

A project should develop a **characteristic value profile --- a
constellation**.

Possible dimensions already identified include:

-   cooperation,
-   ecological impact,
-   economic impact,
-   educational value,
-   resilience,
-   scalability,
-   ethical quality,
-   evolutionary potential,
-   capacity to generate new multisymbiotic relationships.

This list is deliberately incomplete. A central requirement is the
ability to discover and add dimensions that were not included in the
original architecture.

### 3. Information-Carrying Stars

The visual properties of a star could encode different kinds of
information.

#### Color

Possible representation of the type or field of value.

#### Size

Possible representation of the scale or reach of impact.

#### Brightness

Possible representation of maturity, strength of evidence, clarity or
another quality still to be determined.

#### Star Type

Different forms --- for example **ordinary, crystal or rainbow stars**
--- could represent different qualities, structures or combinations of
qualities.

#### Crystal Type

A **Crystal Star** could potentially contain an additional layer of
information.

Different crystalline or gemstone forms --- for example:

-   diamond,
-   ruby,
-   emerald,
-   sapphire,
-   topaz,
-   or other crystalline forms ---

could become visual carriers of different properties or combinations of
properties within the constellation.

At this stage, we deliberately do not assign fixed meanings to
particular crystals.

> **Can different crystal types or crystalline structures become an
> intuitive and meaningful visual language for representing distinct
> qualities, value patterns or combinations of characteristics?**

The crystal system should be developed only if the distinctions it
introduces correspond to meaningful information rather than functioning
as decoration.

#### Number of Arms

Could represent the number of connected vectors, dimensions or forms of
complexity.

#### Position

Could communicate the relationship of one value to other values in the
constellation.

#### Connections

Links between stars could represent dependencies, reinforcement,
conflict, exchange or the generation of additional value.

These mappings are **hypotheses, not specifications**.

### 4. The Project as a Node

A small intervention may create an extraordinary number of valuable
connections. A large project may be powerful but isolated.

Therefore an idea can also be understood as a **node within a network of
consequences and possibilities**.

> **What becomes connected because this idea exists?**

### 5. Value Added by Participants

Participants should be able to **increase a project's value**, for
example by discovering:

-   a missing beneficiary,
-   a new application,
-   an overlooked cost,
-   a potential partner,
-   a new symbiotic relationship,
-   a secondary use for a resource,
-   an unintended consequence,
-   a missing field of knowledge,
-   a way to transform waste into input,
-   a new cultural, educational or social benefit,
-   or an entirely new dimension of value.

The evaluation process can therefore become a **development process**.

> **How could this become more multisymbiotic?**

### 6. Symbiotic Constellation

The **Symbiotic Constellation** would map:

-   participants,
-   beneficiaries,
-   contributions,
-   direct and indirect benefits,
-   costs and risks,
-   dependencies and conflicts,
-   missing connections,
-   reinforcing relationships,
-   secondary effects,
-   new possibilities generated by the arrangement.

The result should be a **shape or constellation of relationships**,
rather than merely a number.

### 7. Time and Scale

The tool should eventually ask how a system changes over one year, ten
years or one hundred years; when ten, a thousand or a million people
participate; when conditions change; and whether benefits, costs,
fragility and future possibilities change with scale.

### 8. Probability Games

**Probability Games** would place proposals into different scenarios and
investigate:

-   where a proposal becomes blocked,
-   where weak points appear,
-   which assumptions fail,
-   which benefits disappear or emerge,
-   where risks accumulate,
-   what alternative versions perform better,
-   which trajectories create the richest field of further
    possibilities.

The objective is not to predict the future perfectly, but to make
proposals **playable, challengeable and improvable before decisions
become irreversible**.

### 9. AI as a Research Multiplier

AI could help:

-   search for analogous systems and precedents,
-   identify stakeholders and missing relationships,
-   search for contradictory evidence,
-   expose assumptions,
-   generate alternative arrangements,
-   suggest additional evaluation dimensions,
-   identify possible multisymbiotic extensions,
-   simulate scenarios,
-   connect knowledge across disciplines,
-   formulate questions the original participants did not ask.

AI should not simply pronounce a final score. Its more interesting role
may be to **expand the field of what can be seen and investigated**.

## WHAT WE DON'T KNOW YET

Among the open questions:

-   **What exactly constitutes a multisymbiotic system?**
-   How do we distinguish multisymbiosis from ordinary cooperation,
    mutual benefit or optimization?
-   Who or what counts as a participant?
-   How should indirect and future beneficiaries be represented?
-   Which dimensions of value should be universal, if any?
-   Which should depend on context?
-   How should uncertainty and strength of evidence be represented?
-   How should conflicting benefits and negative effects appear?
-   Should stars be the primary visual language at all?
-   What should color, brightness, size, type and number of arms encode?
-   What makes a **Crystal Star** different from an ordinary star?
-   What could a **Rainbow Star** represent?
-   Can diamond, ruby, emerald, sapphire, topaz or other crystal types
    meaningfully represent different properties or combinations?
-   Could crystalline structure itself carry information beyond
    traditional gemstone symbolism?
-   How do we avoid an attractive but arbitrary classification?
-   How should time change the constellation?
-   How do we model second- and third-order consequences?
-   Can the system actively propose new multisymbiotic arrangements?
-   How should human judgment and AI analysis interact?
-   How do we prevent the system from becoming a hidden social-ranking
    mechanism?
-   How can participants challenge the criteria themselves?
-   How do we compare constellations without collapsing them back into
    one hierarchy?

> **How do we design a tool capable of discovering kinds of value that
> its designers did not know to include?**

## WHAT NEEDS TO BE BUILT

### Conceptual Foundation

Definitions of multisymbiosis, multisymbiotics, participant, benefit,
value, relationship and increasing mutual benefit.

### Taxonomy of Value

An extensible system for describing different forms of value without
assuming that the initial categories are complete.

### Relationship Model

A method for representing participants, exchanges, dependencies,
reinforcement, conflict and emerging relationships.

### Symbiotic Constellation

A multidimensional representation of the system being investigated.

### Visual Language

Research into stars, colors, brightness, magnitude, shapes, connections,
**crystal types and crystalline structures as potential information
carriers**, and alternative visualization methods.

### Evidence Layer

A way to distinguish claims, assumptions, evidence, uncertainty and
demonstrated outcomes.

### Time & Scale Layer

Methods for examining how the constellation changes across time, scale
and different conditions.

### Probability Games

Scenario testing, simulation and exploration of alternative
trajectories.

### AI Research Layer

Methods through which AI systems can investigate, challenge and expand a
proposal.

### Participation Layer

A system through which researchers, specialists and other participants
can add evidence, criticism, missing connections and proposed
improvements.

### Comparison Layer

Methods for comparing different constellations without reducing them
unnecessarily to a single ranking.

### Governance & Ethics

Rules concerning transparency, manipulation, bias, privacy, contested
values and responsibility for criteria.

### Validation

Methods for testing whether the tool actually helps produce better
understanding and better-designed systems.

-   identifying relevant existing research,

participate.

## Research Invitation

This is **Prototype Architecture v0.1**.

It is deliberately incomplete.

We are publishing it because a visible unfinished structure can be
examined more precisely than a general request for help.

If your research, professional practice or experience intersects with
any part of this architecture, we invite you to challenge it.

Tell us what already exists. Tell us what we have misunderstood. Tell us
which disciplines should be here and are missing. Tell us which
questions are badly formulated.

Or help us build and test the next version.
