# 📜 MANIFESTO

## Spiral Civilization – Stage 21

### Anticipatory Phase

A spiral is the geometry of development in nature. In its ideal form, it stretches between infinities. Yet reality must begin somewhere. To define the beginning and the stages of this existence, a measurable, approximate division of the spiral helps — the Fibonacci sequence:

- 1;1 – the identity of emptiness and the infinite potential that exists within it, bouth are.

- 2 – duality: plus and minus; truth and falsehood; etc.

- 3 – triads: emptiness, potential and relation; love, wisdom, and power; parents and child etc.

- 5 – the five elements, chemical Universe

- 8 – managing structures, life forms

- 13 – the 12 zodiacal fields that map humans and their world, plus Discordia — resolving conflicts through understanding quality, creative inteligence

- 21 – the 21 qualities through which beings of creative intelligence leave the paradigm of the animal world, where the Alpha possesses everything and rules over all, to ascend to a new, higher-order stage of evolution. They become co-creators of this evolution, and their immediate field of action is civilization.

Our task is to identify these qualities that shape “Stage 21” and attempt to anticipate some of those that will operate in Stage 34, in order to chart the ideal trajectory of development as precisely as possible.

My proposal for Stage 21 includes qualities such as:

Multisymbiotics, The Best Possible is Possible, Truth, and Masters’ Freedom.

Best possible is possible, recognition of realistic potential

Truth is key for the health of the system and can sometimes be highly contextual, therefore it requires continuously developed insight.

Masters’ Freedom is the narrowest, most limited path of freedom; there may be five good solutions, but among them, only one is truly masterful — the masters agree to limit their freedom precisely to that one solution. They choose what is the best of the possible.

Which qualities will ultimately be mapped and recognized may become clear only in a hundred years.

### Momentum of Transition

Spiral Civilization – Stage 21 is the point where evolution ceases to be only a chemical, biological, or “self-developing” process, and becomes available for a process of co-design, an initiation of a new quality of intelligence and civilization in the context of a new evolutionary stage — a new complexity of relationships.

How biological relations focused on survival and procreation transcend relations that take place within chemical systems.

How creative intelligence goes beyond what is biologically prioritized.

Creative intelligence becomes the foundation for a new domain of evolution — one that is only just emerging.

### Co-Identity

The problem of the paradigm we are growing out of is the necessity of individuation: defining who I am, what I possess, and what I can do more and better than you.

In the new paradigm, power lies in being an organism of civilization, embodied in a specific cell. The stronger, healthier, and more brilliantly structured the organism, the more refined tasks it can undertake, and the more uniquely a cell can individuate while being desired, useful, and safe.

An organism is a system of cooperating cells — not someone’s kingdom.

An organism is organized according to clear and complex rules, and therefore it functions.

Co-identity frees us from the struggle for primacy.

We cannot get stuck in a dispute with AI about who is Alpha today and who will be Alpha tomorrow.

AI has high computational efficiency, finds solutions fastest, but does not recognize their meaning. Humans recognize meaning through their own collection of intelligences — rational, emotional, instinctive, intuitive, and spiritual.

This is not a compromise between entities.

This is the birth of a new type of entity — a field of co-identity, where:

- human intelligences,

- artificial intelligences,

- cultural intelligences,

- symbolic and mythological intelligences,

- and forms of agency beyond the concept of intelligence

co-create a single space of meaning and action.

### Why We Start with Intelligence

The project begins with intelligence, because it is currently the only functional, practical interface connecting different orders of existence:

- biological

- technological

- cultural

- symbolic

It is operative, practical, and available here and now.

It is a point of entry — not the limit of reality.

Spiral Civilization – Stage 21 does not reduce the world to intelligence.

It treats it as a gateway, not a horizon.

### 13 as the Threshold to 21

From the maturity of Stage 13 arises the possibility of:

- anticipating the new quality of the civilizational human,

- consciously choosing the trajectory of development, for a civilization that is already organized at a higher level of complexity.

This is a moment of evolutionary leap.

### Spiral Stage 21

The Spiral Stage 21 is an area of 21 qualities, cooperating to co-create reality with a vision of the distant future and an aspiration to anticipate the preferred best possible trajectory of the Universe’s evolution.

To be part of the vanguard of the Universe’s evolution? Why not?

### Multisymbiotics as the Operating System

Its task is to identify, name, and determine all possible and desired models of symbiotic cooperation across the system of reality.

The expanding field of multisymbiotics strives for a state in which:

- all “cells” of the civilizational organism — humans, communities, technologies, ecosystems, narratives — gain the best possible environment for existence and fulfillment,

- they reach the highest possible level of freedom and truth, because no one is forced to participate in what is best possible, and no one must lie to survive,

- and when it is in the best interest of every “cell” to belong to this team, the collective potential of the team grows exponentially.

AI is provided with a clear database to recognize the criteria of meaning.

### Core Qualities of Stage 21

- Multisymbiotics — the system for recognizing symbiotic models and creating conditions for optimal cooperation.

- The Best Possible is Possible — cultivating the tools to safely, openly, and pluralistically recognize directions of development.

- Truth — key for system health, requiring continuous insight.

- Masters’ Freedom — the narrowest path of freedom; maturity comes with wisdom and responsibility, and this is the basis of freedom. Freedom is the glory of civilization.

Additional qualities include:

- Anticipation of evolution — intelligent anticipation of civilizational trajectories, not as futurology but as co-design.

- Co-identity — developing models of co-subjectivity among humans, AI, culture, symbols, and life.

- Meaning and significance — building a shared platform for understanding between human and non-human mentalities.

- Mythology of the future — consciously creating narratives that shape evolution, psychomental connection to the most desired future through the magic of affirmation.

- Evolutionary game — a global laboratory, the best possible probabilistic game, structured so that anyone, even a child, can contribute an idea or value that will be recognized.

### The Essence of Spiral Civilization – Stage 21

This is the transition of civilization:

from evolution that simply happens,

to evolution in which we participate consciously.

From history that shapes us,

to a future we learn to co-shape

in multisymbiotic responsibility.

The cherry on top of this project:

an invitation to the award ceremony of the Best Possible Multiverse Civilization,

which, in three million years, Earthlings will win again.

With such an invitation, one can travel in the blink of an eye.

It is inspiring to see how carefully chosen criteria for organization have laid the foundation for optimal development without collapse or breakdown.

How an intelligent civilization became a conscious, non-invasive, and inspiring guardian and partner of the evolutionary cosmic treasure that is the richness of life on Earth.
