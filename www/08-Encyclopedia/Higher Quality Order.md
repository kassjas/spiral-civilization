# Higher Quality Order

## Canonical Definition

**Higher Quality Order** describes not a higher level of complexity, but a higher quality of organization within a given level of complexity.

Different orders can exist at the same civilizational level while producing radically different trajectories — from destructive, exploitative and corrupt structures toward increasingly intelligent, lawful, noble and multisymbiotic ones.

Higher Quality Order asks which forms of organization move from Zero toward the Best Possible.
