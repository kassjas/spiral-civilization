# Free-Flow Improvised Dictation (FFID)

## Canonical Definition

**Free-Flow Improvised Dictation (FFID)** is the working method through which most of the ideas presented in Spiral Civilization were originally developed. Rather than writing finished texts, I think aloud. Ideas emerge while I speak, often in a nonlinear form, before they are organized, revised, and eventually transformed into authorized publications.
