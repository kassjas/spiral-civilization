## How do we understand evolution and the place of civilization within its trajectory?

### Anticipatory Phase

A spiral is the geometry of development in nature. In its ideal form, it stretches between infinities. Yet reality must begin somewhere. To define the beginning and the stages of this existence, a measurable, approximate division of the spiral helps — the Fibonacci sequence:

- **1; 1** – the identity of emptiness and the infinite potential that exists within it
- **2** – duality: plus and minus; truth and falsehood
- **3** – triads: parents and child; love, wisdom, and power
- **5** – the five elements; the physical Universe
- **8** – managing structures; life forms
- **13** – the 12 zodiacal fields that map humans and their world, plus Discordia — resolving conflicts through understanding quality
- **21** – the 21 qualities through which beings of creative intelligence leave the paradigm of the animal world, where the Alpha possesses everything and rules over all, to ascend to a new, higher-order stage of evolution. They become co-creators of this evolution, and their immediate field of action is civilization.

Our task is to identify these qualities that shape **Stage 21** and attempt to anticipate some of those that will operate in **Stage 34**, in order to chart the ideal trajectory of development as precisely as possible.
