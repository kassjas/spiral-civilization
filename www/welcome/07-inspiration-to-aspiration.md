Inspiration to Aspiration
A mythology of the future is not a prophecy.
It is an affirmed possibility placed on the distant horizon — an anchor for imagination, aspiration and choice.
By imagining where we most deeply want to arrive, we can begin to recognize which direction to take today, and how even the smallest step may lead towards that future.
