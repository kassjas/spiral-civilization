Topics in this Free Flow
Declaration of Civilized Status
The idea of an application to a future Forum of Civilized Civilizations / Cultures of the Multiverse.
A civilization cannot merely call itself civilized; it should be able to describe and demonstrate its condition.
The application becomes a multidimensional description of what a civilization has achieved and what direction it is taking.
The Best Possible as a navigational direction
We know something about where we are.
We can consciously ask where we want to go.
The Spiral can continue indefinitely once its geometry of development is established.
„Best Possible” therefore needs tools, categories and methods of evaluation rather than one fixed definition.
The geometry of Spiral Evolution
Civilization can deliberately prepare a developmental geometry.
Organization itself becomes an expression of intelligence and beauty.
The question is not only how much civilization possesses, but how intelligently it organizes complexity, resources, freedom, relationships and future possibilities.
Earth three million years in the future
The long temporal perspective functions as a thought experiment.
Earth could remain extraordinarily alive and diverse millions of years from now.
Civilization could minimize unnecessary intervention while protecting the conditions in which life can continue evolving.
Life as the Great Treasure
Earth’s biosphere is the result of billions of years of evolutionary experimentation.
Life is imagined as a precious intelligent systems laboratory, database and evolutionary experiment.
A mature intelligence should protect its continuation rather than consume or simplify it.
The Great Gate of Maturity
Somewhere in the far future there is a threshold to a higher level of universal complexity.
A Guardian stands at the Gate.
Entry requires a maturity examination.
The fundamental question is: How did you care for the Treasure of Life entrusted to your planet?
The condition of Earth’s life forms becomes part of the civilization’s „ticket.”
Forum of Civilized Civilizations
Humanity/Earthians eventually become capable of applying for participation in a larger field of civilizations or cultures.
The application must be evidence-based: express yourself as well as you can and show what you actually became.
The future prize / attractor
A playful future scenario: Earthians win the Best Possible Civilization Field in the Multiverse.
„Earthians are the best! Let’s party!”
The future celebration works as an attractor from which present decisions can be viewed backwards.
Bridges of entanglement with the future
Present actions can be imagined as creating bridges toward a future civilization millions of years ahead.

Multisymbiotic cooperation
Search for relationships that multiply possibilities for multiple entities.
Explore different kinds of symbiosis between people, AI, ecosystems, cultures and other forms of intelligence.

Zero
The point of presence.
Attention directed toward what is before conceptual construction.
A private experiential field: just be / not be / presence.

Zero and Infinity

Freedom with responsibility
Greater freedom should be accompanied by greater maturity.
Advanced intelligence may demonstrate its development partly through its ability not to exercise unnecessary control.
Guardian Angel principle
Protect.
Assist where necessary.
Intervene as little as possible.
Preserve the independent evolutionary potential of what is being protected.
The question of evil / opposition
What is opposition for?
What can intelligence learn from it?
Can destructive forces be understood sufficiently to build systems capable of moving very far toward their opposite: increasing possibility, freedom, responsibility and flourishing?
