This appendix audits the **Phase 1B architecture Cross-Reading**, not a completed direct Cross-Reading of all four original 21-question responses. A source location establishes what was written; it does not prove the proposal works. No item is validated as Type 4 emergence. Type 2 attribution is restricted to distinctive formulations in this corpus, not worldwide originality.

Line references are one-based in the UTF-8 files identified by the full SHA-256 hashes below. Hashes identify preserved bytes; they do not establish truth, authenticated identity or identical provider execution. Findings identify the originating formulation, later response and present analyst's classification separately. A/B/C/D validation means source/chronology/relation/epistemic category. External validation is a separate question.

The complete raw Cross-Reading remains in the project's internal research archive. The public appendix exposes locators, short descriptions and source hashes, not raw exchanges or private operational packets. It enables identification of the evidence; readers without the internal texts cannot independently reproduce every comparison from this public appendix alone. Access to fuller source material would require a separate release decision.
