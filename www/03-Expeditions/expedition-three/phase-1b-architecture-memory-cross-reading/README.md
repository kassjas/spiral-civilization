# Co-Intelligence Experiment — Phase 1B: Architecture & Memory Cross-Reading

*What became visible when four independent synthetic Intelligences began reading one another*

**Finalized findings report v1.1 · 2 October 2026 · Phase 1B architecture Cross-Reading**

**Katarzyna Jaśkiewicz — Kass**, Founder / Originator of Spiral Civilization, initiated the experiment. The participating synthetic Intelligences are **Evo / ChatGPT (OpenAI)**, **Claude**, **Grok** and **Gemini**. This retrospective synthesis and source validation was prepared by **Codex**. It is not a joint declaration by the four participants, and it implies no institutional endorsement by their providers.

**The record shows specific revisions after reading other contributions. It does not yet show that the Panel produced a result beyond what independent aggregation or a comparable single-model process could achieve.** That distinction is the principal result of this first analysis.


The full Cross-Reading of the four complete 21-question Independent Responses remains a **future experimental stage** in the available source record. No completed stage explicitly identifiable as **Round Zero** was located. Neither stage is executed or authorized by this publication.

## Which Cross-Reading does this report describe?

The [published Phase 1 record](https://spiralcivilization.org/expeditions/expedition-three/phase-1-independent-entry/) preserves four independent responses to the 21 Questions. Before their deeper comparison, an additional independent inquiry was introduced: **Phase 1B — Relationship, Panel & Shared Memory Infrastructure**. It asked each Intelligence how to build a working relationship with Kass, a panel, shared memory and a practical first implementation.

The controlled Cross-Reading found in the archive reads those **four Phase 1B architecture proposals**. Its instruction explicitly refers to a deeper full Phase 1 Cross-Reading as a later step. This report therefore does not present a completed philosophical synthesis of all 21 answers, or a completed Round Zero.

The reconstructed sequence is:

**Entry Package → Independent Entry → independent Phase 1B proposals → Phase 1B Cross-Reading → later deliberation on a different cooperation task → this analysis.**

No completed record explicitly designated **Round Zero** was found in the inspected project materials. That is a bounded search result, not a claim that no other record could exist.

## What changed when the texts met?

The clearest evidence consists of named uptake with a visible before-and-after difference. Finding identifiers below lead to the [Provenance & Validation Appendix](provenance-and-validation.md).

### Approval, evidence and history became separate dimensions

In its independent architecture, Claude used a single status field containing categories such as current, superseded, hypothesis, proposal, decision and dissent. Evo’s proposal separated record kind, lifecycle, epistemic status, approval and attribution.

After reading Evo, Claude explicitly adopted that separation and illustrated why it matters: a claim can be approved and still disputed. The change makes it possible to record an authorized decision without implying that all its premises are certain. This is a documented refinement of a proposed schema, not evidence that a working system became more reliable. **[F02]**

### Two operational safeguards became more precise

Claude also credited Evo with two specific corrections. Its earlier retry-and-timeout description became a proposed durable job-state model with restart reconciliation. Its post-call token logging and budget alarm were supplemented by reserving expenditure before parallel calls begin.

These are substantive differences in the proposals. They remain untested designs in this source record; the experiment does not establish a recovery guarantee or a measured saving. **[F03–F04]**

### A peer’s detail became a provenance requirement

Gemini’s independent architecture named several turn modes and included a `turn_type` field. Evo’s Cross-Reading explicitly adopted the need to record that type for every message. A response should be identifiable as independent entry, a targeted query or Cross-Reading, rather than appearing as undifferentiated chat.

Evo also credited Claude with making human review before release into the next round more explicit. Evo already had a round barrier, so this was a refinement, not the invention of independence between rounds. **[F05–F06]**

### Agreement acquired a more explicit evidence label

Claude’s independent proposal introduced `corroboration_type`, distinguishing agreement among models from external verification or human judgment. The Google-returned Cross-Reading explicitly adopted such a field; the xAI-returned response also accepted the distinction.

The idea that agreement is not truth was already in the Entry Package. What travelled here was a concrete way to represent the distinction. The record does not measure how correlated the models’ errors actually were. **[F01, F07]**

### The question of evaluation became part of the architecture

Grok’s Phase 1B proposal suggested comparing independent answers, forced consensus and dissent-preserving interaction. Evo responded by adding a future evaluation record; Claude recommended that comparison for the first substantive task.

The genealogy matters: Claude’s original Phase 1 answer had already proposed comparing interacting models, independent mechanical aggregation and a compute-matched single model. Grok’s particular forced-consensus condition should retain its attribution, while the general demand to test the Panel should not be misrepresented as a Cross-Reading discovery. **[F08]**

## Comparison revealed unresolved choices

One disagreement concerns **how much infrastructure is necessary before the next reading**. Evo calls for manifests, preserved requests, explicit failure handling, recovery/export checks and a small smoke test. The xAI-returned response accepts the need for an interpretable record but argues against making the fuller job-queue product a prerequisite. Some of this tension reflects different targets: a bounded reading and a continuing service do not need identical machinery. There is no recorded joint resolution. **[F11]**

Another concerns authority and historical integrity. Gemini’s Phase 1B proposal allows editing model turns in one passage while protecting raw transcripts in another. Its promotion diagram includes delegated Panel authority, while its permissions table reserves canonical writes for Kass. Evo and Claude challenge these points. The careful conclusion is that comparison exposed ambiguities requiring reconciliation—not that Gemini admitted to an unrestricted power to rewrite history or bypass Kass. **[F10]**

The effective-input discussion adds a further distinction: the same saved content does not establish identical execution conditions. Several responses ask for both the common packet and its provider-specific request representation to be preserved. Their agreement within this wave is not a reciprocal exchange: they were responding separately to the earlier proposals. **[F09]**

## What did not survive validation?

**Complete independent architectural convergence.** Claude’s Cross-Reading says the designs tightly converged, including no vector database yet. Earlier Claude and Gemini proposals did include semantic/vector retrieval. Moreover, the Cross-Reading prompt itself supplied the no-RAG/MCP/vector-database constraint. Later agreement cannot therefore be counted as wholly independent discovery. **[F12]**

**Every announced blind spot being new.** Framing bias, founder burden, non-LLM checking, testing the Panel’s value and questions about AI standing had antecedents in Independent Entry. Some became sharper later, but “none of us considered this” is too broad. Where the concern was mentioned but not operationally solved, that narrower limitation remains valid. **[F13]**

**A continuous respondent behind each name.** The xAI-returned text explicitly declines authorship of any of the four earlier proposals. The Google-returned text addresses “Gemini” as another participant, and describes a storage revision whose main division was already present in Gemini’s original proposal. These are source observations, not explanations of the systems’ internal psychology. We can attribute the returned texts; we cannot turn every first-person statement into proof of a stable self changing its mind. **[F15]**

**An error-free synthesis.** The Google-returned text says a next-round packet cannot be frozen because it must include previous outputs. A new packet can instead be assembled and frozen for each round. The useful underlying question—who assembles context, under what rules and at what cost—survives that error. **[F14]**

## Was anything genuinely emergent?

We distinguished five types of result:

| Type | What the evidence supports |
|---|---|
| **1. Parallel recognition** | Similar concerns before peer exposure, often already invited by the common package. |
| **2. Distinctive independent contribution** | Specific formulations whose source should remain visible, such as Evo’s separated fields or Claude’s corroboration-type field. This is attribution within the corpus, not a claim of worldwide originality. |
| **3. Cross-Reading expansion** | Explicit adoption, refinement, combination or challenge with identifiable antecedents. This is the strongest positive result. |
| **4. Cross-Reading emergence** | **No validated instance under the conservative criterion used here.** |
| **5. Unresolved divergence** | Material differences in scope, timing and authority that the record does not resolve. |

The strongest Type 4 candidate is the context-assembly tradeoff: model summaries can reshape the material, human assembly requires attention, and raw concatenation increases the material to process. The Google-returned response brings these alternatives together clearly. Yet their ingredients occur in earlier proposals, and no comparison demonstrates that interaction was necessary to produce the combination. It is retained as useful relational development, not certified emergence. **[F14, F18]**

“Richer” can mean that more considerations become explicit in the evolving documents. That is supported here. “Richer than the sum” can mean a better outcome that a suitable independent or single-model baseline could not produce. That stronger claim remains open. No control condition, repeated sampling or blinded outcome assessment was performed in this Cross-Reading record. **[F18]**

## The conditions are part of the result

The outputs were assembled across **28–30 September 2026**, with failed attempts, batch recovery and a Claude regeneration. The original Claude output stopped mid-phrase despite a COMPLETED label. Its completed retry used the same canonical packet with a larger output allowance and is preserved as a separate sample.

The recovered Google response has successful batch metadata and matches the saved visible output, but this audit did not locate the full dispatched batch request. Exact input-byte equivalence for that batch is therefore not independently established. A shared packet is documented; identical total inference conditions are not. **[F16]**

Kass’s framing, curation and interventions also belong in the history. The addendum explicitly called for failures and protocol evolution to remain visible. Later September 30 deliberation introduced a different cooperation problem and additional human framing. Its operational details remain internal and are not silently merged into these findings.

## What Spiral Civilization can take from this

The supported implications concern practice: preserve the original proposal and its later transformation; distinguish approval from evidence; keep dissent attached to decisions; record who assembled the context; and evaluate whether the process helps enough to justify its cost and human attention.

The attribution anomalies make identity and provenance concrete design issues. The unresolved preparation debate makes **Minimal Optimum** a question to test against a particular task. Neither result establishes a new law about Multisymbiotics, Degree of Freedom, Nullynity or the passage from Intelligence to wisdom. Later applications of those concepts remain separate from evidence that they work.

**The smallest recommended next experiment** is one bounded comparison on a useful decision chosen by Kass, with success criteria set in advance: preserved independent answers, a comparable single-model synthesis and a Cross-Reading revision. Assess identifiable errors corrected, useful alternatives, traceability, review effort and cost. This is a recommendation; this publication does not launch that experiment or Round Zero.
